About me

I’m a final-year B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Lakireddy Bali Reddy College of Engineering, Mylavaram. I like projects that connect real data to working software, from a Spring Boot chat application to a message-passing neural network that predicts crystal energies.
